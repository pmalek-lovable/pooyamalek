About

A designer who kept noticing the same thing.

Most good designers aren't stuck on skill. They're stuck on clarity. Fixing that turned out to be the work I care about most. Pooya

For thirteen years I've designed products people use to book flights, find homes, and buy things online. Now I spend part of my time helping other designers figure out what they're missing.

I'm a Product Design Lead and Functional Lead at Eurowings Digital, where I own the partner and ancillary experience across post-booking, a portfolio of six-plus products. I co-built the design team there from five to twenty-plus people and shipped systems adopted across multiple integrations.

Along the way I kept noticing the same thing. Most designers who are genuinely good at their craft have no idea how their career actually works. They're doing strong work but can't articulate their impact. They're ready for senior but don't know how to demonstrate it. They're stuck not because of skill, but because of clarity.

So I started helping. As an ADPList Top 50 Mentor with twenty-plus mentees, as Lead Instructor at UserX Academy across four cohorts and a hundred and twenty-plus students, and through writing on Substack, I help mid-career designers understand what senior actually means and build a clear path to get there.

If you're a designer trying to level up, or a company building a design function in a consumer or travel product, I'd like to hear from you.
